Good to know

Questions, answered.

Can I self-host Relay?

Yes — our Team and Enterprise plans include a VPC deployment option, so pipelines run entirely inside your own cloud account.

Is there a free plan?

Every account starts free — up to 10k events a month, no card needed.

What if a connector I need is missing?

Build one against our open connector SDK, or ask us — most requests ship within a week.

What happens if I go over my plan's limit?

We queue and deliver everything anyway — you'll just see an upgrade prompt on your dashboard. Nothing ever silently drops.

Can I write custom code in a step?

Drop a JavaScript function into any step for transforms the visual builder can't express.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes, from your billing settings — no calls, no forms, effective at the end of the current cycle.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything business owners usually ask before starting a project with codexaman.

01 What services does codexaman offer?

codexaman is a software development, digital marketing and website development team. We build websites (HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, WordPress, React), mobile apps (Flutter, React Native), e-commerce stores (Shopify, WooCommerce), and also handle logo design, Google My Business setup, Google Maps integration, local SEO and social media profile setup — so your online presence is handled end to end by one team.

02 How long does a website take to build?

Most Starter and Business plan websites are delivered in 3–7 working days, depending on the number of pages and how quickly content and approvals come in. Apps, e-commerce stores and custom platforms take longer, and we share a realistic timeline upfront during the discovery call — never a rushed guess.

03 Do you offer support after the website goes live?

Yes. Every plan includes 6 months of free minor changes, no revision fees, and lifetime query support — our team stays reachable for questions and small fixes long after launch, not just until the invoice is paid.

04 Can you help my business show up on Google Maps and local search?

Yes — this is one of our core services. We set up and verify your Google My Business profile, integrate Google Maps into your website, and run local SEO so your business ranks for searches people in your city are actually making, not just generic keywords.

05 Do you also handle social media profiles and logo design?

Yes. We set up and brand your Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn profiles to match your website, and every plan includes a free basic logo so your visual identity is consistent everywhere your business shows up online.

06 Do you work with businesses outside India?

Yes — codexaman is based in Kolkata, India, and works with businesses across the globe. We collaborate async over email, WhatsApp and video calls, accept international payments, and adjust working hours to overlap with your time zone where needed.

07 How much does a website cost?

Pricing starts at ₹5,999 for a portfolio or landing page and ₹8,999 for a full business website of up to 10 pages, one-time, with no hidden fees. Apps, e-commerce stores and custom platforms are quoted based on scope — see the pricing section above or request a free quotation.
